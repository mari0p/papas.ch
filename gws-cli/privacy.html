Hermes Workspace Assistant: Privacy

Hermes Workspace Assistant is a personal tool used only by Marios Papas to read and organise his own Google Workspace data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides).

  • It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.
  • Data accessed through Google APIs is processed on the owner's own computer and by the AI assistant he uses under his employer's enterprise agreement (Anthropic Claude). It is not sold, and not shared with anyone else.
  • It never sends email. It creates drafts that the owner reviews and sends himself.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
